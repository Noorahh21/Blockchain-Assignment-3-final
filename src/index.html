About this dApp

This is the same voting contract from Assignment 2, but now it is compiled and deployed with Truffle on a local Ganache blockchain instead of Remix and Sepolia.

  • The owner (Ganache account 0) adds candidates
  • Every account can vote only once
  • Votes are stored on the local blockchain
